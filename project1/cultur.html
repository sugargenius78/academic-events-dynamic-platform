🎭Cultural Day Festival

📅 Date: May 3, 2025
🕒 Time: 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM
📍 Location: University Garden & Amphitheater
🎓 Organized by: Student Union & Cultural Clubs

🔍 Description:

Travel the world without leaving campus!
Cultural Day celebrates global diversity through music, dance, food, and traditional fashion from over 15 different countries.
Students are invited to perform, cook, or present their culture in vibrant, interactive booths.

🎉 Highlights:

  • Live performances & international dances
  • Food tasting from various cuisines
  • Cultural fashion show & photo booth
